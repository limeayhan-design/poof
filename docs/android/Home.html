FAQ
How do I send a file?
Open the Send tab, pick a type (photo, doc, clipboard), select a recipient, then swipe the central circle upward.
Are my files encrypted?
Yes. All transfers go peer-to-peer via WebRTC. Secure (Premium) adds an extra AES-256 layer on the file itself.
Why isn't my file sending?
Check that your recipient is online (green dot) and Poof is open on both sides. On Free, Poof stays limited to your local Wi-Fi.
How do I pair a new device?
Send tab → tap an empty slot → Add device sheet → scan the QR or enter the code shown on the other device.
How does Premium work?
Premium unlocks Secure, Track, Boost, Offline and Studio. 7-day free trial, cancellable from iCloud Settings.
Can I recover an expired file?
No. Secure files with expiry are removed locally + from the Photos gallery if saved. That's the « auto-destruct » promise.
Where do I see my send history?
Onglet Received → tap sur « All history » pour voir Sent / Received / Clipboard, avec la date et le destinataire.
