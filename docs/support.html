Support

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Contact us

Email Limeayhan@icloud.com. We usually reply within 48 hours. You can also tap Settings → Contact support in the app.

How do I add a device?

Install Poof on both devices. On the Send tab, tap + and pick a method: nearby devices, scan a QR code, send an invitation, or enter a code manually.

How do I send a file?

Pick a photo or a file on the Send tab, choose the device, then swipe up. The progress shows in the Dynamic Island, and the file appears in the Received tab of the other device.

How big can a file be?

  • Free: up to 100 MB per file
  • Premium: up to 500 MB per file
  • Offline mode (devices nearby, no internet): up to 1 GB per file

The first send of the day is slow. Why?

Our relay server can take a few seconds to wake up after a quiet period. The next sends are fast.

Restore or cancel Premium

To restore a purchase, open the Premium screen and tap Restore. To cancel a subscription, go to iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Poof.

Delete my account

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. Your account and its data are removed from our server.

Privacy

Files sent between Apple devices running Poof are end-to-end encrypted. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
