Frequently asked.

Is Poof really cloud-free?

Yes. Files travel directly device-to-device via WebRTC data channels. Our servers only help two devices find each other during the initial pairing — after that, everything is peer-to-peer. On the same LAN, we don't even do that.

Is Poof open source?

Yes, MIT license. Core protocol, apps and desktop clients are on GitHub. You can audit the encryption, self-host the signaling server, or contribute.

How is it different from AirDrop?

AirDrop only works between Apple devices. Poof works across iPhone, Mac, Windows, Linux — any combination. Same simplicity, no Apple lock-in.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly and annual plans cancel in one click. Lifetime is a one-shot payment — you own it forever, no subscription.

Why is the iOS price higher?

Apple takes 30% commission on in-app purchases. On our website we pay ~3% and pass most of the difference to you. Same features, cheaper on desktop.

When does Devium launch?

Devium launches shortly after Premium. Drop your email in the "Notify me" button and you'll get early-bird pricing.

What happens if my Wi-Fi drops during a transfer?

Nothing bad. Poof tracks how much has been received. When the connection comes back, the sender asks "where were we?" and resumes from the exact chunk — no re-upload, no corruption.
